We welcome questions from researchers, facilities and collaborators who want to
explore how SciLifeLab OMERO can support the management and sharing of their
imaging data. If you are unsure whether your use case fits, get in touch anyway
and include a short description of your project and your data.

Write to us at [omero@scilifelab.se](mailto:omero@scilifelab.se).

<a class="btn" href="mailto:omero@scilifelab.se" role="button">Email the OMERO team</a>

### When to get in touch

- You want to know whether OMERO is a good fit for your imaging data
- You are planning a new project and need guidance on access
- You work at a SciLifeLab facility and want to discuss workflow integration
- You need help understanding how users, projects and datasets can be organized
- You are already using the service and something is not working as expected

Applying for access is a separate process, described on
[How to get access]({{< relref "get-access" >}}).
