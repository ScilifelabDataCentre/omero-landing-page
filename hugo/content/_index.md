Welcome to SciLifeLab’s prototype bioimage data management service. Built on the globally recognised, open-source platform OMERO, the service offers access-controlled, active data storage where users can collaboratively organize, visualize and annotate their bioimaging data sets during the active stages of their research projects. Data organized in the SciLifeLab-hosted OMERO instance can be accessed from local and national HPC resources. The service also supports the use of FAIR data management best practices to facilitate submissions to public end repositories for image data later on. 

The service will be accessible to both SciLifeLab image data producing infrastructure units to handle user projects and deliver data, as well as researchers from the Swedish life science community and their collaborators. Please see here for more information on how to get access. 

If you already have access to an allocation on the prototype service, you can log in to SciLifeLab’s OMERO server here.
