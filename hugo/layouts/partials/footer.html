About SciLifeLab OMERO

The SciLifeLab OMERO service is developed and maintained by the DDLS Data Science Node in Cell and Molecular Biology (DSN-CMB) team as part of the SciLifeLab Data Platform, operated by the SciLifeLab Data Centre. Members of the DSN-CMB team are affiliated with SciLifeLab Data Centre and Chalmers e-Commons, based at Chalmers University and Uppsala University. This service is supported by SciLifeLab and the Knut and Alice Wallenberg foundation through the Data-Driven Life Science (DDLS) program, as well as by the Swedish Foundation for Strategic Research (SSF).
