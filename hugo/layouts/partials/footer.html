About SciLifeLab OMERO

The SciLifeLab OMERO prototype service is developed and operated by the SciLifeLab Data Centre. Members of the team are affiliated with SciLifeLab Data Centre and Chalmers e-Commons, based at Chalmers University and Uppsala University. This service is supported by SciLifeLab and the Knut and Alice Wallenberg foundation through the Data-Driven Life Science (DDLS) program, as well as by the Swedish Foundation for Strategic Research (SSF).
