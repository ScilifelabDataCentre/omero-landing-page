{{- /* A single capability card, used inside the "cards" shortcode. icon: a bootstrap-icons class name, for example "bi-cloud-upload". title: the card heading. url: optional. Makes the whole card a link to that target. docs: optional. Same, for a path inside the documentation, resolved against site.Params.docsURL. Shortcode arguments are literal strings and cannot call the "docs" shortcode, hence the separate argument. The icon repeats the title visually, so it is hidden from screen readers. The link goes on the title rather than around the whole card, so that its accessible name is the title alone instead of the title plus the body text. The card is still clickable across its full area, through an overlay drawn on the link in .scilife-feature-card-linked. */ -}} {{- $url := .Get "url" -}} {{- with .Get "docs" }}{{ $url = printf "%s%s" site.Params.docsURL . }}{{ end -}}
