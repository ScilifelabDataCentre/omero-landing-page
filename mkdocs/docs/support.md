# Getting help

Where to take a question, depending on whether it is about OMERO itself or about
the SciLifeLab service.

## Questions about using OMERO

OMERO is developed by the Open Microscopy Environment, and their documentation is
more thorough than anything we could restate here.

- The [OMERO guides](https://omero-guides.readthedocs.io/) walk through importing
  data, working with Fiji, napari and CellProfiler, and using the APIs.
- The [OMERO documentation](https://omero.readthedocs.io/en/stable/users/) covers
  the clients and the command line interface.
- The [image.sc forum](https://forum.image.sc/tag/omero) is where the OME team and
  other OMERO users answer questions.

For general questions about how to do something in OMERO, the forum will usually
get you a faster and better answer than we can.

## Questions about the SciLifeLab service

Write to [omero@scilifelab.se](mailto:omero@scilifelab.se) for anything specific
to this deployment:

- access, allocations, renewals and extensions
- your group, its members and its permissions
- storage quota
- something that is not working as expected

## Reporting an outage

The same address. There is no separate incident channel and no status page.
SciLifeLab OMERO is a prototype, so maintenance can happen without advance notice.

## What to include in a request

- what you were trying to do
- which client you used, and its version: a browser, OMERO.insight, Fiji, a script
- the name of your group, and the project or dataset involved
- the exact error message, or a screenshot
- roughly when it happened

Do not include credentials or personal data in an email to us.

## Response times and office hours

`omero@scilifelab.se` is a shared mailbox read by the OMERO team at SciLifeLab,
not a ticketing system. We work Swedish office hours, Monday to Friday between
09:00 and 17:00, and aim to reply within a week. Support is best effort.

## Problems with the website or this documentation

Both are built from a public repository. Open an issue in
[omero-landing-page](https://github.com/ScilifelabDataCentre/omero-landing-page).
That repository is not a support channel for the service itself.
